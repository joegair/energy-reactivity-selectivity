Assumptions

R = 1.9872 × 10⁻³ kcal mol⁻¹ K⁻¹. Transmission coefficient κ = 1 throughout. ΔG° and ΔG‡ are taken as temperature-independent, so changing T holds the energy fixed and re-solves the ratio, rate, or selectivity. Real ΔG values drift with T whenever ΔS is non-zero.

Panel 1 assumes two states with equal degeneracy. Panel 2 assumes a single irreversible first-order step. Panel 3 assumes the two paths share one intermediate and the branching step is product-determining; the same expression covers the classic Curtin–Hammett case of two rapidly interconverting conformers, where ΔΔG‡ is measured between the two absolute transition-state energies.
